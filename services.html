Facial Services

Personalized care. Lasting results.

The Lana Society experience is more than a treatment — it's a moment to slow down. Our facial services are designed to meet your skin where it is and take you where you want to go, with thoughtful, personalized care and a space that feels like your own.

The Lana Society Experience

The treatment is what we do. The feeling is what they remember.

Lana Society services are designed around one simple belief: luxury is not a style or a price — it is a feeling. Every treatment should feel personal, intentional, and effortless. Guests should feel that they can leave everything at the door, take a breath, and settle into a space where both their skin and their experience are thoughtfully cared for. Our services are designed to meet your skin where it is and help reveal where it can go — an opportunity to restore, refine, and invest in healthier, more radiant skin that evolves with you.
